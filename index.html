Kirana Cloud

2025

Solo builder · open source, MIT

Inventory and billing for neighbourhood stores. Works offline and syncs when the network returns.

  • Conflict-free sync engine merges edits from multiple staff devices without a server round-trip, even after days offline.
  • Barcode scanning and GST-compliant invoices run entirely on-device; a receipt prints in under 3 seconds on cheap Bluetooth printers.
  • Used weekly by ~900 shops across Pune and Bengaluru; localised into Hindi, Marathi and Kannada.
  • Battery-friendly design: <2% drain per 8-hour shift, tested on ₹6,000 Android devices.

Waypoint

2024

Side project → 12k monthly users

A trip planner that works as an installable web app, with shared itineraries and offline maps.

  • Vector tiles are pre-cached per itinerary, so day-two in the Himalayas still renders maps at 60 fps with no signal.
  • Real-time collaborative editing with presence cursors, built on Supabase Realtime row subscriptions.
  • Ships as a PWA on iOS, Android and desktop from one codebase — the whole client is under 120 KB gzipped.
  • Trending on Hacker News and r/travel; 1,900 GitHub stars and 40 community route packs within a year.

Contract build for an independent publisher
